Every rule in a script gets a relatable student situation ("7 months in and a cheaper college down the road?") and the
concrete impact ("a new visa granted outside Australia — not guaranteed"), not just the rule stated.

**Why:** the user asked for this on Episode 4 (2026-10-27): "be sure to give examples that would be relatable and the impact".

**How to apply:** scenarios are fictional (allowed by the brief) but each must trace to official wording or an official
example (e.g. Home Affairs' Bachelor → Diploma example, Standard 7's brochure/placement case); never invent outcomes
the source doesn't state (e.g. consequences of a breach). Pair with [[feedback-visual-per-sentence]] and
[[feedback-go-deep-not-surface]].
