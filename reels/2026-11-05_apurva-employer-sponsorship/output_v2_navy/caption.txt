An employer agreeing to sponsor you is great news, but it doesn't make you visa-ready. There are three important parts to consider: the employer must meet the relevant sponsorship requirements, the nomination must meet the applicable occupation, salary and nomination requirements, and you must meet the visa requirements, including relevant skills, English criteria and experience.
