Tushar Chopra: 189 round, occupation by occupation

A split-screen reel built from Tushar's seven takes (P1–P7). The top half is one continuous motion-graphics take synced to his words. The bottom half is Tushar. Each topic gets its own visual style. One sheet of paper carries through every style change, so the reel flows instead of cutting like slides.

Sound: low music bed with 8 cues at most (the true-peak limit). Captions: 2-word gold-highlight band on the seam. Timings are on the cut timeline and will shift by under 0.1 s after the base build. Mocks are simplified sketches of each panel, not final frames.
